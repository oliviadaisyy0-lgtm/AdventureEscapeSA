About Adventure Escape SA

Adventure Escape SA was founded by Liam Daniels, who has always had a passion for outdoor adventure and eco-tourism. After years of guiding weekend hikes for friends and family, Liam noticed that many families, tourists, schools and corporate groups struggled to find one place where they could compare and book exciting outdoor activities.

Today, Adventure Escape SA brings together a variety of guided outdoor experiences that encourage teamwork, fitness and a love of nature from mountain hikes to corporate teammbuilding days.
